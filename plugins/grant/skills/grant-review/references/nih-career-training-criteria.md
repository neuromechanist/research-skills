# NIH Career Development and Training Award Review Criteria

Review criteria for K-mechanism career development awards, F-mechanism fellowship awards, and T-mechanism training grants. These differ substantially from R-mechanism research grants in both criteria weighting and reviewer focus.

> **Scope note.** The three-factor **Simplified Review Framework** (NOT-OD-24-010) in `nih-review-criteria.md` applies to **Research Project Grants (RPGs)** only; it is **not** applied to K, F, or T mechanisms. Track the mechanism-specific changes separately, all effective for due dates on or after 2025-01-25:
> - **Fellowships (F30, F31, F32, F33, F99/K00)** were revised under **NOT-OD-24-107** into **three** new scored criteria (Candidate's Preparedness and Potential; Research Training Plan; Commitment to Candidate). The F31/F32 section below reflects these.
> - **Institutional training grants (T-series, e.g. T32)** were revised under **NOT-OD-24-129**. The T32 section below still describes the pre-2025 structure; treat it as historical and verify current criteria against the notice and the specific NOFO.
> - **Individual career development awards (K)** did **not** have their scored review criteria changed for 2025 (only reference-letter/application instructions were updated); the K sections below remain current.

## Key Differences from R-Mechanism Grants

- **Candidate/Applicant quality** is a primary scored criterion (not just "Investigator(s)")
- **Mentoring plan** is a scored criterion; mentor track record carries significant weight
- **Career development or training plan** is scored separately from the research plan
- **Research plan** is typically shorter and evaluated for training potential, not just scientific merit
- **Environment** emphasizes training resources, not just research infrastructure
- Scoring uses the same 1-9 scale but with different descriptors appropriate to each mechanism
- Study sections may include reviewers with specific career development expertise

## K99/R00 (Pathway to Independence Award)

Designed for postdoctoral researchers transitioning to independent faculty positions. The K99 phase (mentored) leads into the R00 phase (independent).

### Scored Review Criteria

#### 1. Candidate
- Scientific background and training relative to career stage
- Publication record and evidence of productivity
- Evidence of growing independence during postdoctoral training
- Commitment to a research career in a relevant field
- Realistic timeline for transition to independence

#### 2. Career Development Plan / Career Transition Plan
- Quality of the K99-phase mentored training activities
- Clarity of skills to be acquired during the K99 phase
- Specificity of the transition timeline (K99 to R00)
- Plan for identifying and securing an independent position
- Evidence that the plan fills genuine gaps in the candidate's training

#### 3. Research Plan
- Scientific merit of both K99-phase and R00-phase research
- Logical progression from mentored (K99) to independent (R00) aims
- Feasibility of the K99 research within the mentored environment
- R00-phase research must be independent from the mentor's program
- Appropriate scope for a new investigator launching a lab

#### 4. Mentor(s), Co-Mentor(s), Consultant(s)
- Mentor's track record in training independent investigators
- Quality of the mentoring plan (meeting frequency, milestones, evaluations)
- Complementary expertise of co-mentors or consultants
- Letters of support with specific commitments (not generic)
- Mentor's willingness to support the candidate's independence

#### 5. Environment and Institutional Commitment
- Availability of training resources (courses, seminars, core facilities)
- Institutional commitment to the candidate's development (protected time, space)
- Access to collaborators and intellectual community
- For R00 phase: evidence that the receiving institution will support the transition

### Additional Review Criteria (Not Scored Separately)
- Protections for human subjects (if applicable)
- Vertebrate animals (if applicable)
- Data management and sharing plan
- Budget and period of support

## K08 / K23 (Mentored Career Development Awards)

K08 targets clinician-scientists pursuing basic/translational research; K23 targets patient-oriented research. Both support protected time (typically 75%) for research training.

### Scored Review Criteria

#### 1. Candidate
- Clinical and research training to date
- Potential for a productive independent research career
- Publication record appropriate to career stage
- Commitment to dedicating adequate effort to research (minimum 75% for most K awards)
- Need for additional mentored research experience (justification for K vs. R)

#### 2. Career Development Plan / Career Goals and Objectives
- Clarity of short-term and long-term career goals
- Specific training activities planned (coursework, workshops, techniques)
- Logical connection between training activities and career goals
- Timeline with milestones for achieving independence
- Plan for transitioning to R01 or equivalent independent funding

#### 3. Research Plan
- Scientific merit of the proposed research
- Appropriateness as a training vehicle (the research should build the skills outlined in the career development plan)
- Feasibility within the K award period
- Evidence that the candidate, not the mentor, designed the research
- Potential for generating sufficient preliminary data for an R01 application

#### 4. Mentor, Co-Mentor(s), Consultant(s)
- Mentor's research qualifications and funding status
- Mentor's track record of training K awardees who became independent investigators
- Quality and specificity of the mentoring plan
- Advisory committee composition and role
- Letters of support demonstrating genuine commitment (specific plans, not boilerplate)

#### 5. Environment and Institutional Commitment
- Institutional commitment to the candidate's career development
- Protected time guarantee (letter from department chair)
- Access to appropriate patient populations (K23) or laboratory resources (K08)
- Availability of relevant courses, seminars, and training programs
- Plans for laboratory space and support during and after the award

### Scoring Note
- Reviewers evaluate whether the candidate truly needs mentored career development support
- A candidate who appears ready for R01 may be considered overqualified for a K award
- Conversely, a candidate who lacks fundamental skills may need more preliminary training before a K award

## F31 / F32 (Ruth L. Kirschstein NRSA Individual Fellowships)

F31 supports predoctoral (PhD) candidates; F32 supports postdoctoral researchers. Both emphasize training potential over independent research productivity.

### Scored Review Criteria (revised fellowship framework, NOT-OD-24-107)

For due dates on or after 2025-01-25, individual fellowship applications (F30, F31, F32, F33, F99/K00) are reviewed under **three** scored criteria rather than the legacy five. The substance of the old criteria is folded in as sub-bullets under the new criterion it now informs.

#### 1. Candidate's Preparedness and Potential (formerly: Applicant + Training Potential)
- Academic record and evidence of research aptitude
- For F31: prior research experience, coursework, and potential for a research career
- For F32: doctoral training quality, publications, and readiness for the proposed training
- Commitment to a biomedical, behavioral, or clinical research career
- Demonstrated qualities (scientific understanding, creativity, perseverance) and capacity to progress to the next career stage
- Appropriateness of the proposed training for the applicant's career stage

#### 2. Research Training Plan (formerly: Research Training Plan + Sponsors/Environment adequacy for the plan)
- Scientific merit, rigor, and feasibility of the proposed research as a training vehicle
- Alignment of the plan with the candidate's developmental goals and identification of the skills to be developed
- Clarity of the research goals and experimental approach
- For F31: project complexity should be appropriate for a graduate student
- For F32: project should develop skills beyond those acquired during doctoral training
- Whether the sponsor(s), environment, and institutional resources adequately support the plan (coursework, seminars, core facilities, monitoring of progress)

#### 3. Commitment to Candidate (formerly: Sponsors, Collaborators, and Consultants + Institutional Commitment)
- Strength and appropriateness of the mentoring plan for the trainee's needs
- Sponsor's (primary mentor's) research competence, funding, and track record of training predoctoral (F31) or postdoctoral (F32) researchers
- Qualifications and roles of co-sponsors, collaborators, and consultants
- Whether organizational/institutional commitment sufficiently aligns with the training objectives and will enable career advancement

> **Historical note.** Applications reviewed **before 2025-01-25** used five scored criteria: Applicant; Sponsors, Collaborators, and Consultants; Research Training Plan; Training Potential; Institutional Environment and Commitment to Training. That structure may still be relevant when reading an older summary statement.

### F31 Diversity Supplement Considerations
- For F31 applications from individuals from underrepresented groups, reviewers also consider the potential to enhance diversity in the biomedical research workforce

### Scoring Note
- The emphasis is on training potential, not just research quality
- A mediocre research plan in an outstanding training environment may score better than an outstanding research plan with a weak mentoring plan
- Sponsor's commitment to training (not just research productivity) is weighted heavily

## T32 (Institutional Training Grants)

T32 supports predoctoral and postdoctoral research training programs at institutions. Unlike individual awards, the T32 evaluates the program, not a single trainee.

> **Revised framework (NOT-OD-24-129).** Institutional training grant applications with due dates on or after 2025-01-25 use an updated review structure. The criteria below describe the **pre-2025** structure and are retained for historical context; for a current T32 review, verify the criteria against NOT-OD-24-129 and the specific NOFO before scoring.

### Scored Review Criteria (pre-2025 structure; see the note above)

#### 1. Training Program
- Quality and coherence of the overall training program design
- Didactic and experiential training components
- Curriculum structure (core courses, electives, rotations)
- Program-specific milestones and expectations
- Integration of responsible conduct of research (RCR) training
- Plan for addressing rigor and reproducibility in training
- Recruitment strategy and timeline

#### 2. Training Program Director / Principal Investigator
- PD/PI's qualifications and leadership experience
- Track record of mentoring trainees in the relevant area
- Administrative capabilities and time committed to the program
- Vision for the training program's direction and growth

#### 3. Preceptors / Training Faculty
- Research competence and funding status of participating faculty
- Breadth and complementarity of faculty expertise
- Faculty track record of trainee productivity (publications, grant applications)
- Faculty-to-trainee ratio and availability for mentoring
- Diversity of research approaches represented by the faculty

#### 4. Institutional Environment and Commitment to Training
- Institutional resources dedicated to the training program
- Quality of research facilities and shared resources
- Institutional commitment (matching funds, space, administrative support)
- Breadth of the intellectual environment (seminars, journal clubs, retreats)
- Opportunities for interdisciplinary collaboration

#### 5. Program Outcomes (Renewal Applications)
- Career outcomes of former trainees (faculty positions, research careers)
- Publication and grant records of trainees during and after the program
- Time to degree completion (predoctoral) or time to first independent position (postdoctoral)
- Retention of trainees in research careers
- Diversity of the trainee pool and outcomes for underrepresented trainees
- For new applications: present a plan for tracking and reporting outcomes

### T32 Special Considerations
- T32 applications are typically large and complex, involving multiple faculty and trainees
- Reviewers evaluate the program holistically, not individual faculty grants
- The training plan must add value beyond what trainees would receive through individual mentoring
- Appointment criteria and selection process for trainees are evaluated
- Plans for trainee career development beyond research skills (communication, teaching, leadership) are valued

## Scoring Differences Summary

| Aspect | R-Mechanism | K-Mechanism | F-Mechanism | T32 |
|--------|-------------|-------------|-------------|-----|
| Primary focus | Research quality | Career development + Research | Training potential + Research | Program quality |
| Preliminary data | Expected (R01) | Expected but less extensive | Not expected for F31; minimal for F32 | N/A (program-level) |
| Mentor evaluation | Not scored separately | Scored criterion | Scored criterion | Faculty scored as group |
| Candidate evaluation | Part of Investigator(s) | Primary scored criterion | Primary scored criterion | PD/PI scored separately |
| Environment emphasis | Research infrastructure | Training resources + Protected time | Training breadth + Mentoring | Institutional commitment |
| Research scope | Full research program | Training-appropriate scope | Training-appropriate scope | N/A (program-level) |
| Typical score drivers | Approach + Significance | Candidate + Mentor | Applicant + Sponsors | Program + Faculty + Outcomes |
