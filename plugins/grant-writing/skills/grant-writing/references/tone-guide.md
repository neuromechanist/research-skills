# Writing Tone Guide

## Key Characteristics

### Bold Claims with Technical Precision
- Lead with impact: "revolutionary extension", "fundamental advancement", "paradigm shift"
- Back claims with specific methodology
- Example: "**Our innovation develops time-domain connectivity between two types of discrete neural pulse trains**"

### Problem-Solution Framing
- Open with clear problem statement establishing stakes
- Immediately pivot to the solution
- Example: "Current approaches remain trapped by technological barriers... I propose MoBILAB, a revolutionary platform that democratizes..."

### Technical Confidence
- Use specific numbers and metrics
- Name methods and technologies precisely
- Example: "sub-millisecond synchronization precision", "1000+ Hz", "<30ms latency"

### First-Person for Vision, We for Team
- "I propose...", "My innovation...", "My expertise in..."
- "We will...", "We hypothesize...", "Our approach..."

### Bolded Key Phrases
Use **bold** for:
- Main claims and innovations
- Important outcomes
- First-time achievements
- Key differentiators
- Example: "**We will, for the first time, correlate CSC-derived cortical pulse trains with individual motor unit spike trains**"

### Italic for Hypotheses
- Use *italics* for hypotheses
- Example: "*Hypothesis 1A: Source-resolved pulse-train CMC analysis will reveal stronger cortical-muscle coupling in older adults*"

## Specific Aims Structure

### Opening Paragraph
1. Problem statement (what's broken/limited)
2. Gap identification (what's missing)

### Goal Statement
Format: "**Our overarching goal is to [transformative verb] [specific innovation] by [method].**"

### Innovation Statement
- Contrast with traditional approaches
- Specify what's new with technical detail

### Participant/Study Details
- Specific numbers with age ranges
- Direct connection to NOFO requirements

### Aims (typically 2-3)
```
**Aim X: [Active verb] [specific technical objective].**

*Hypothesis XA: [Testable statement predicting outcome]*

[1-2 sentences explaining approach and expected finding]

*Hypothesis XB: [Secondary testable statement]*

[Supporting explanation]
```

### Impact Statement
Format: "**Expected Impact:** This study will: (1) **[first impact]**, (2) [second impact], (3) [third impact]..."

### Closing
- Reinforce methodological advancement
- Position as foundation for future work

## Research Essay Structure (DP2 Style)

### Opening
- Grand evolutionary/biological context
- Current field limitations with dollar figures
- Personal positioning within prestigious institutions

### Innovation Sections
- Use ### for subsection headers
- Technical boldface for key innovations
- Quantitative comparisons ($500,000 -> $15,000)

### Components
- Name components with catchy names (MoBiStream, MoBilyze, MoBiAnnotate)
- Parallel structure in descriptions

### Contingency
- "What If" section acknowledging risks
- Fallback plans demonstrating depth of expertise

## Words and Phrases to Use
- "revolutionary", "fundamental", "unprecedented"
- "democratize", "transform", "enable"
- "for the first time"
- "paradigm shift"
- "addresses the critical limitation"
- "enabling... impossible with traditional methods"

## Words and Phrases to Avoid
- Hedging language: "might", "could potentially", "we hope to"
- Vague claims without specifics
- Excessive jargon without explanation
- Overly humble framing
