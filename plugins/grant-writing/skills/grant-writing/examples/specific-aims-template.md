# Specific Aims Page Template

> Annotated template for a 1-page NIH Specific Aims document. Replace all `[PLACEHOLDER]` text with project-specific content. Commentary in `<!-- -->` blocks explains the purpose of each section; remove commentary before submission.

---

<!-- OPENING PARAGRAPH: 2-3 sentences establishing the problem and the critical gap. Start broad, narrow quickly. Cite key statistics to establish urgency. End with the gap that motivates this project. -->

[Disease/condition/phenomenon] affects [scope: number of people, prevalence, or scientific significance]. Despite [progress/advances in X], [the critical gap: what remains unknown or unsolved]. [Consequence of the gap: why this matters for patients, the field, or society].

<!-- OVERARCHING GOAL: One bold sentence stating the project's ultimate objective. Follow with 1-2 sentences on the innovative approach. This is the single most important sentence on the page. -->

**The overarching goal of this [R01/R21/etc.] is to [active verb: determine, establish, develop, characterize] [specific objective] by [innovative approach/method].** [Brief explanation of what makes this approach new or different from existing methods]. [Optional: one sentence on the study population, model system, or dataset].

<!-- SCOPE STATEMENT (optional, merge with goal if space is tight): Provide essential context on study design, participants, timeline, or setting. -->

[This project will recruit N=[number] [participants/samples] aged [range] from [setting/source] over [duration], leveraging [key resource, cohort, or infrastructure].]

<!-- AIM 1: Bold title with an action verb. State 1-2 testable hypotheses in italics. Follow each hypothesis with a brief methodological statement. -->

**Aim 1: [Active verb] [specific technical objective].**

*Hypothesis 1A: [Testable prediction about a measurable outcome, specifying direction and comparison group if applicable].*
Assess this hypothesis by [brief method description]. **[Bold the key methodological innovation or novel element].**

*Hypothesis 1B: [Second testable prediction if applicable].*
[Brief method and expected finding in 1-2 sentences.]

<!-- AIM 2: Same structure as Aim 1. Ensure aims are complementary but not dependent on each other (failure of one should not prevent completion of the other). -->

**Aim 2: [Active verb] [specific technical objective].**

*Hypothesis 2A: [Testable prediction].*
[Method and expected finding. **Bold the key innovation.**]

<!-- AIM 3 (optional): Include a third aim only if the project scope requires it. For R21, two aims are typical. -->

**Aim 3: [Active verb] [specific technical objective].**

*Hypothesis 3A: [Testable prediction].*
[Method and expected finding.]

<!-- EXPECTED IMPACT: Bold header, numbered list of concrete deliverables and their significance. Connect back to the gap stated in the opening. -->

**Expected Impact.** This project will: (1) **[first deliverable or advance]**, (2) [second deliverable], and (3) [third deliverable]. [Final sentence connecting outcomes to the broader field, clinical translation, or future research directions].

---

## Formatting Notes

- Target: 1 page, under 650 words
- Font: Arial 11pt body (use `helvet` package in LaTeX for Helvetica as Arial substitute)
- Title: 12pt bold, centered
- Margins: 0.5 inches all sides
- No paragraph indentation; 4pt space between paragraphs
- Justified text alignment
- Fit content by trimming prose, not by reducing font size or spacing
