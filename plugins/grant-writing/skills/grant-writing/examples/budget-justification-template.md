## Notes

- For modular budgets, round total direct costs up to the nearest $25K module
- Account for ~3% annual salary increases in multi-year budgets
- Verify the current NIH salary cap before finalizing PI/Co-I salary requests
- Fringe benefit rates must match the institutional rate agreement
- Indirect cost rates must match the federally negotiated rate agreement
